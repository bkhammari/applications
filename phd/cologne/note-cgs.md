# Cologne Graduate School (CGS), structured PhD route

Per Prof. Krause, the University of Cologne recruits its PhD students through the
Cologne Graduate School in Management, Economics and Social Sciences (CGS), and
admissions run only in spring.

Implications for timing. The informal route now is direct contact with individual
faculty (Siegloch, Pinger, van Maarseveen) about supervision and openings. The
formal route is the CGS structured-program application in the spring cycle. A
supervisor who is already interested strengthens that application considerably,
so the direct emails come first.

To do when the spring call opens:

1. Check the CGS and WiSo doctoral program page for the exact deadline and
   required documents (transcripts, CV, proposal, references, language proof).
2. Line up references early (Anna Person, plus Schabert, Krause, or possibly
   Pinger).
3. Reuse the research proposal and academic CV already in this repo.
