## Why him, and why this was missed

Carina Neisser answered a cold email on **17 July 2026** with four names for
applied microeconomics at Cologne: Sebastian Siegloch, Pia Pinger, Sonja Settele
and Johannes Wohlfart. Three of those four are now in motion. Wohlfart was never
contacted, and the recommendation sat unused for eight weeks.

He is worth the email on the merits. He is at Cologne and in ECONtribute, he works
on belief formation and expectations, and he co-authors with Settele and
Christopher Roth, including in the American Economic Review: Insights. That is the
literature the perceived-returns part of the proposal rests on, so the fit is
specific rather than generic.

He also joined ECONtribute from CEBI Copenhagen at the same time as Settele, which
makes him a second route to Copenhagen alongside her.

Neisser's referral is worth naming in the first line. A colleague's suggestion
opens a cold email much better than a paper citation does.

Subject: PhD inquiry: beliefs, education access and labor market inequality

Dear Professor Wohlfart,

My name is Baha Khammari. I am completing my M.Sc. in Economics at the University of Cologne (expected September 2026) and am looking for a PhD position in applied microeconomics. Carina Neisser suggested I get in touch with you.

My master's thesis (grade 1.0) estimated the causal wage effects of Brazil's ProUni scholarship program using the Callaway and Sant'Anna (2021) staggered difference-in-differences estimator on roughly 5 million RAIS observations across 558 microregions. The main result is a non-monotonic dose-response that conventional two-way fixed effects estimation reverses in sign.

My proposed PhD compares which higher education access mechanism, scholarships, loans, or quotas, most reduces labor market inequality, and for whom. Part of it rests on how prospective students perceive the returns to higher education, which is where your work on belief formation and expectations connects most directly, and it is the part I would most like to develop further.

Would you have twenty minutes to discuss whether there is a fit, either for the Cologne Graduate School round or in your group? I have attached my CV and a research proposal.
