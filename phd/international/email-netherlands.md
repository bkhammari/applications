## Maastricht, and the thing to know before applying

Maastricht is the only foreign option you could take without moving, at about 90
minutes from Düsseldorf. Before applying, note how their process actually works.

**GSBE applicants are expected to have identified a supervisor and a funded
position before they apply.** So an application submitted cold into the portal is
not the main route, it is the paperwork that follows the main route. This is the
same structure as a German chair E13 post, described in
[`../e13-direct.md`](../e13-direct.md), and the same email works for it.

Practical detail. Files are reviewed on a rolling basis through May 2027 for the
September 2027 intake, and Studielink opens on 1 October 2026, so there is no cliff
edge tomorrow. External candidates send a CV and a one-page motivation letter,
naming the staff they would want to work with, to
`gsbe-phd-application@maastrichtuniversity.nl`.

So the order is: pick two or three names at SBE whose work fits, write to them
directly, and use the central address as a fallback or in parallel. Naming the
people you want to work with is required by the central route anyway, so the
research is not wasted either way.
