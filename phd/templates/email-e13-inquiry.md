# E13 position inquiry, direct to a chair

The point of this template is that it asks a **different question** from the
supervisor cold email. That one asks for a meeting about your career, and the
cheapest honest reply is "apply through the graduate school." This one asks
whether a funded position exists, which a professor can answer in one line.

Keep it short. Six sentences is plenty. The CV does the arguing.

**Subject lines that work**, because they say what the email is:

- `Wissenschaftlicher Mitarbeiter position, M.Sc. Economics available from October`
- `Enquiry: funded position at your chair from October 2026`

**Language.** German for German chairs unless their site is in English. Both
versions below. German reads as less presumptuous for a Haushaltsstelle at a
smaller university.

**Attach** the academic CV. Offer the thesis and proposal rather than attaching
them, since a first email with three PDFs sometimes bounces.

My CV is attached, and I am glad to send the thesis and a research proposal.

Thank you for your time.

## Variant for someone who already knows you

For Roth, Liesenfeld, Schabert, or anyone who taught you. Replace the opening,
keep everything else.

> Dear Professor [X],
>
> I took your [course] in [year] and finished it with [grade]. I am completing my M.Sc. this month and looking for a position as a wissenschaftlicher Mitarbeiter from October 2026, with a doctorate alongside.
>
> Do you have, or do you expect, a funded position at your chair?

## What not to do

Do not ask for a meeting in the first email. It raises the cost of replying, and a
professor with no position will say no to a meeting who would have said "not now,
but ask again in spring" to a direct question.

Do not ask whether they would supervise a PhD. That is channel one, and the answer
is the graduate school.

Do not attach three PDFs. One CV, and offer the rest.
