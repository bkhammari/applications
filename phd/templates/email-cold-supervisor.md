# Cold email to a potential PhD supervisor, base template

The pattern all the tailored supervisor emails follow. Keep it short: who you
are, what you work on, why them, and a clear ask. Attach CV and proposal.

**Adapt every bracket.** The `[why this professor]` line is the one that matters
most. Name a specific paper, method, dataset, or policy focus. Never send it
blank or generic.

---

Subject: PhD inquiry: [your field, for example applied microeconomics or labor economics]

Dear Professor [X],

My name is Baha Khammari. I am completing my M.Sc. in Economics at the University of Cologne (expected September 2026) and am looking for a PhD position in [field]. [Optional: how you came to write, a referral, an event, a paper of theirs.]

My master's thesis (grade 1.0) estimated the causal wage effects of Brazil's ProUni scholarship program using the Callaway and Sant'Anna (2021) staggered difference-in-differences estimator on roughly 5 million administrative observations, finding effects that are strongly heterogeneous by ethnicity and region. My proposed PhD compares which higher education access mechanism, scholarships, loans, or quotas, most reduces labor market inequality, and for whom.

[Why this professor specifically, one or two sentences tying their work to yours.]

Would it be possible to meet to discuss potential PhD opportunities in your group? I have attached my CV and a research proposal and can provide a thesis abstract on request.

Best regards,
Baha Khammari
M.Sc. Economics (expected Sep. 2026), University of Cologne
