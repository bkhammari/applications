# Getting RCT experience, and whether it is worth getting

Written 18 September 2026, after the Tilburg posting was rejected for wanting
*"demonstrated hands-on experience implementing randomized controlled trials in
low- or middle-income countries"*.

The question was whether a research assistant position exists that would supply
that. It does. Whether to take it is a strategy decision, not a filler decision,
and this file exists so that it gets made deliberately.

## The short version

**The route is real and it has a name.** J-PAL and IPA Research Associate posts are
the pipeline that produces exactly that sentence on a CV. Both run continuous
hiring.

**It is field-based, and it is not a bridge.** These are one to two year commitments
in Kenya, Uganda, the DRC, Sierra Leone, India or Bangladesh. That breaks the
Rhine-Ruhr and Frankfurt geography this whole search is built on, and it moves the
doctorate from October 2027 to 2028 or 2029.

**There is a chicken-and-egg at the door.** IPA's own Research Associate spec asks
for *experience managing field staff in developing countries*, alongside a master's,
Stata and survey programming. The honest entry point below that is a Field Research
Intern or a Research Coordinator post.

## Route A: the real thing, if the field is acceptable

| Where | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **J-PAL** | *General Field Research Associate* and *General Data Research Associate* are both open as standing pools, plus a field RA and a field intern in the DRC | `povertyactionlab.org/careers`. The general pools are the way in when no named project fits |
| **IPA** | Research Associate, country offices including Kenya and Uganda | Asks for a master's in economics or a quantitative social science, Stata, data management and survey programming, and field staff management experience |
| **IDinsight, Busara (Nairobi), World Bank DIME** | Same shape | Worth scanning if the decision goes this way |

The data-side J-PAL role matters here. A **Data Research Associate** is closer to
what he already does, cleans and analyses trial data rather than running the
fieldwork, and it is the least painful way into the world.

## Route B: impact evaluation without leaving the geography

This buys development-evaluation credibility and does **not** buy the Tilburg
sentence. Be honest about which one a given application needs.

| Where | Why | Geography |
|---|---|---|
| **C4ED, Mannheim** | A non-profit that runs large-scale RCTs and quasi-experimental evaluations in more than 40 countries, sitting alongside the Mannheim econometrics chair. The closest thing in Germany to an RCT shop | Mannheim, beyond the Cologne commute but on the Frankfurt line |
| **DEval, Bonn** | The federal institute that evaluates German development cooperation. Rigorous methods, and thirty minutes from Cologne | **In geography** |
| **KfW Entwicklungsbank**, evaluation unit | Evaluates KfW development finance | Frankfurt, **in geography** |
| **GIZ**, evaluation unit | Same building group as the internship already applied for | Bonn, **in geography** |

**On DEval's vacancies.** Their public careers page carries only the standing
student-assistant and intern postings. Real staff vacancies go through **Interamt**,
`interamt.de/koop/app/trefferliste?partner=269`, which is where to look. One
*Evaluator\*in* post was advertised for fragile statehood, conflict prevention and
good governance. Recruiting contact is Lisa Weinert, `jobs@deval.org`.

**On C4ED.** Their jobs page renders in JavaScript and returns nothing to a plain
fetch, so it has to be opened in a browser. `info@c4ed.org` for a direct enquiry,
and the E13 template applies: ask whether a position exists or is expected, which is
answerable in one line.

## Route C: fix the gap that actually exists

`CLAUDE.md` records two real gaps. One is no confirmed reference letters, which is
in hand. The other is **no research-assistant experience at all**. That is the gap,
and it is not the same as no RCT experience.

A standard economics pre-doc or RA post at a chair or institute closes the real gap,
keeps the geography, keeps the October 2027 start, and plays to what he already has
rather than retooling away from it. Five million linked employer-employee records, a
heterogeneity-robust staggered DiD, a sign reversal against naive TWFE, and BigQuery
are scarce. The E13 channel and the Dutch vacancy channel both want that.

**predoc.org is a channel not yet in use.** It lists pre-doctoral positions with
named sponsoring researchers, including European ones. Recent listings included UPF
Barcelona (Eeckhout, macro and labour), Zurich (Voth), and CEMFI Madrid (Puga). Also
`econjobmarket.org/market` for the RA and pre-doc section.

## The recommendation

**Do not retool toward field RCTs on the strength of one rejected advert.** The
Tilburg posting was a project position whose requirements came from its grant, not a
signal about the market. Chasing it means two years abroad and a doctorate two years
later, to acquire a method he has not otherwise shown any intention of using.

**If development economics is genuinely the direction**, and the Future Rural Africa
interest and the Development Economics coursework suggest it might be, then Route A
is the honest way in and it should be chosen for that reason rather than as a
reaction. Say so, and the whole plan gets rebuilt around it.

**Otherwise Route C, with Route B as the development-flavoured version of it.** DEval
in Bonn is the single best fit on geography, subject and institution type, and it is
the one to test first.
