# Blue Book Traineeship (European Commission)

A five-month paid traineeship inside the European Commission. Around 1,000 places
per session, two sessions a year (March and October starts).

## Dates for the March 2027 session

Applications run from 22 July 2026 to **4 September 2026, 10:00 Brussels time**.
The session itself runs 1 March to 31 July 2027. The deadline is firm and the
online form closes at the stated time, so aim to submit a few days early.

## Eligibility

Open to university graduates of any discipline. You need a completed degree by
the application deadline, knowledge of two EU languages (one at C1, a second at
B2), and no more than six weeks of prior experience in any EU institution, body,
agency, or delegation. The M.Sc. (expected September 2026) needs to be far
enough along to count, so confirm the degree-completion requirement against the
current notice before relying on eligibility.

Three strands exist: administrative for university graduates (ADMIN UG),
administrative for vocational graduates (ADMIN VET), and translation (TRA). The
ADMIN UG strand is the match here.

## What the application asks for

The application is a single online form on the Blue Book portal. It collects
personal and education data, language levels, and a motivation section, and it
asks you to indicate preferred Directorates-General. There is no separate letter
to upload at the application stage, so the motivation fields and the DG choices
carry the weight.

## Directorate-General preferences

Pick DGs where an economics and policy-evaluation profile is a direct fit. Strong
candidates are ECFIN (economic and financial affairs), EMPL (employment and
social affairs), EAC (education and culture), COMP (competition), GROW (internal
market), and the Joint Research Centre. List a few rather than one, and match
each to something concrete in the research record, for example EMPL and EAC for
the work on higher education access and labour market outcomes.

## Draft motivation text

Adapt the wording to the form's fields and length limits. No em dashes, no
semicolons, plain sentences.

> I am an economist finishing an M.Sc. at the University of Cologne, with research
> on how public policies shape labour market inequality. My master's thesis, graded
> 1.0, used administrative microdata and modern causal methods to evaluate a higher
> education access policy and its effects across regions and demographic groups. I
> want to bring that training in evidence and policy evaluation to the Commission's
> work on employment, education, and economic policy. Two years at PwC Germany gave
> me experience turning large datasets into clear analysis for decision-makers, and
> I work in German and English, with French at B1. A traineeship in a DG such as
> EMPL, EAC, or ECFIN would let me contribute quantitative analysis while learning
> how EU policy is designed and delivered from the inside.

## Checklist

1. Create or update the profile on the Blue Book portal before the window closes.
2. Confirm the degree-completion rule for a September 2026 M.Sc. against the notice.
3. Draft the motivation fields from the text above, cut to the form's limits.
4. Choose four to six DGs and note the fit for each.
5. Have the academic CV ready in case a stage asks for it.
6. Submit by 2 or 3 September, ahead of the 4 September cut-off.
