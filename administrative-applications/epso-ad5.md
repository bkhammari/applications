# EPSO AD5 Generalist Competition (EPSO/AD/427/26)

The entry-grade competition for permanent administrator posts across the EU
institutions. Success puts you on a reserve list from which the institutions
recruit.

## Status and dates

Applications for this cycle ran 5 February to 10 March 2026 and are closed, so
this track assumes you already registered under EPSO/AD/427/26. If you did not
apply, the next opportunity is a future AD5 notice rather than this one.

The competition runs in two stages with an eliminatory cut between them.

1. First stage, reasoning tests: verbal, numerical, and abstract reasoning,
   expected in late October or early November 2026.
2. Second stage, expected in 2027: EU knowledge, digital skills, a situational
   competency test, and the EUFTE written essay.

Supporting documents must be scanned, uploaded, and linked to the EPSO profile
by **7 October 2026, 12:00 Brussels time**. Missing that upload can invalidate
an otherwise strong application, so treat it as a hard deadline in its own right.

## Preparation plan for the reasoning stage

The first stage is computer-based reasoning under time pressure, and it rewards
practice on the specific EPSO question formats rather than general aptitude.

1. Verbal reasoning: passage-based logical inference, watching for statements
   that go slightly beyond what the text supports.
2. Numerical reasoning: data tables and percentages under a tight per-question
   clock. Speed on arithmetic and ratios matters more than advanced maths.
3. Abstract reasoning: pattern sequences. The most trainable of the three with
   repeated practice.

Work through timed mock sets, track accuracy by question type, and drill the
weakest type. Two months of regular practice before late October is a reasonable
target given the September and mid-September commitments elsewhere.

## Fit

The quantitative training behind the thesis, comfort with data under time
constraints from the PwC years, and working German plus English cover the core
demands of the AD5 profile. The numerical section in particular overlaps with
skills the research record already demonstrates.

3. Run timed reasoning practice from now to the test window.
4. Note the test date once EPSO confirms the slot, and plan around the Blue Book
