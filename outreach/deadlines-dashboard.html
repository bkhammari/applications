Düsseldorf — DGSE / DICE

Closes 16 September 2026 · starts January 2027

The Düsseldorf Graduate School of Economics runs an off-cycle January intake, so this is the one application to finish now — months before the German winter round even opens. No GRE, and the fit is real: Schildberg-Hörisch (economics of education) and Südekum (regional labor). Tailored emails are already drafted in outreach/duesseldorf/.

No GRE Master's in econ / quant 2 reference letters Apply via HHU portal

To submit

  • Application form — statement of goals, career plan, research interests
  • Academic CV — reuse cv/academic
  • Official transcripts — B.Sc. + M.Sc., translated if needed
  • Two letters, emailed separately by referees to dgse@uni-duesseldorf.de

Barcelona & France

Less standardized — rolling admission or supervisor-led. Timing approximate for the 2027 cycle.

Barcelona

BSE ecosystem · suggested by Sonja Settele

UB School of Economics · Universitat de Barcelona Mar–Apr 2027

Own PhD, two spring application windows, no GRE. The most accessible of the three — a realistic good-chance entry point.

Good chance
UAB · IDEA · Autònoma de Barcelona Rolling → spring

International Doctorate in Economic Analysis, co-organized with the Barcelona School of Economics.

UPF PhD Track · Pompeu Fabra Opens fall 2026

Entered through the BSE Master in Economics & Finance, whose first year is PhD year one. GRE required; the most competitive route.

Reach

Common path: BSE Master → PhD at UPF/UAB. Decisions run in rounds from late 2026 into spring 2027 — early rounds help.

France

Three good-chance picks · supervisor-led

AMSE · Aix-Marseille Spring–summer 2027

Applied micro, labor, and development with funded doctoral contracts. Best fit and more accessible than PSE/TSE — the top pick.

Good chance · best fit
Paris-Dauphine – PSL · LEDa Spring–summer 2027

Good chance
Paris-Saclay · RITM Spring–summer 2027

Large, well-funded, international selection. Broad applied economics — confirm a supervisor whose work matches before committing.

French PhDs run on 3-year contrats doctoraux tied to a supervisor; secure the advisor first. PSE and TSE are stronger reaches. You have French B1 (DELF); coursework is in English.
