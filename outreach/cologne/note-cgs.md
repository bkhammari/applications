# Cologne Graduate School (CGS) — structured PhD route

Per Prof. Krause: the University of Cologne recruits its PhD students through
the **Cologne Graduate School in Management, Economics and Social Sciences
(CGS)**, and admissions run **only in spring**.

**Implication for timing:**

- The **informal route now** is direct contact with individual faculty
  (Siegloch, Pinger, van Maarseveen) about supervision and openings.
- The **formal route** is the CGS structured-programme application in the
  spring cycle. A supervisor who is already interested strengthens that
  application considerably, so the direct emails come first.

**To do when the spring call opens:**

- Check the CGS / WiSo doctoral programme page for the exact deadline and
  required documents (transcripts, CV, proposal, references, language proof).
- Line up references early (Anna Person; plus Schabert / Krause / possibly
  Pinger).
- Reuse the research proposal and academic CV already in this repo.
