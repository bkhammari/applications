My name is Baha Khammari. I am completing my M.Sc. in Economics at the University of Cologne (expected September 2026) and am looking for a PhD position in applied microeconomics, with a focus on the economics of education and inequality.

My master's thesis (grade: 1.0) estimated the causal effect of Brazil's ProUni scholarship program on formal-sector wages, applying the Callaway & Sant'Anna (2021) staggered difference-in-differences estimator to ~5 million administrative observations. I found that wage gains accrue disproportionately to ethnic minorities in a way conventional methods mask. My proposed PhD compares which higher education access mechanism — scholarships, loans, or quotas — most reduces labor market inequality, and for whom.

Your work on how socioeconomic status shapes skill formation and educational outcomes addresses the upstream side of the inequality my project traces into the labor market; combining the two perspectives would strengthen the causal chain from access to outcomes.
