# Policy / political internship — cold email template

**Where to send:** federal ministries (BMWK, BMAS, BMF), the Bundesbank, IZA,
OECD, EU institutions and MEP offices, economic think tanks and foundations,
political party foundations (e.g. Konrad-Adenauer, Friedrich-Ebert), and
Bundestag/Landtag MP offices. Good as a bridge that builds policy exposure
alongside the research track.

**Adapt:** the `[organisation]` and the one-line motivation paragraph.
