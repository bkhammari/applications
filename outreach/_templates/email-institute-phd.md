# Doctoral inquiry to a research institute / structured program — base template

For institutes and structured doctoral programs (RWI, ZEW, DIW, ifo, and
similar) rather than a single supervisor. Ask about current or upcoming
positions and fit; attach CV + proposal.

**Adapt:** the `[institute / program]` and `[research group / unit]` lines.

---

Subject: Doctoral position inquiry — [institute / program]

Dear [Name / Recruiting Team],

My name is Baha Khammari. I am completing my M.Sc. in Economics at the University of Cologne (expected September 2026) and am looking for a doctoral position at [institute], ideally within its structured program [name, e.g. RGS Econ / CDSE / MGSE / DIW GC].

My work evaluates how public policies shape labor market inequality using causal microeconometric designs on administrative data — a fit for [research group / unit]. My master's thesis (grade: 1.0) applied the Callaway & Sant'Anna (2021) staggered difference-in-differences estimator to ~5 million administrative observations to estimate the wage effects of Brazil's ProUni scholarship program, finding heterogeneous effects by ethnicity and region that conventional methods mask. My proposed PhD compares which higher education access mechanism — scholarships, loans, or quotas — most reduces labor market inequality, and for whom.

I am writing to ask whether there are current or upcoming doctoral positions for which my profile would fit, and about the timeline for the next application cycle. I have attached my CV and a research proposal, and can provide a thesis abstract or transcripts on request.
