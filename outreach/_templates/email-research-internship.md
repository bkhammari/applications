# Research internship / RA / pre-doc — cold email template

**Where to send:** research institutes (RWI, ZEW, DIW, ifo, IZA) and individual
chairs looking for research assistants or pre-doctoral researchers. Good as a
bridge while PhD applications are in flight — and RA work at these institutes is
a common on-ramp to their doctoral programs.

**Adapt:** the `[institute / chair]` and the one-line fit paragraph.

---

Subject: Research assistant / pre-doctoral position inquiry

Dear Professor [X] / Dear [Institute] Team,

My name is Baha Khammari. I am completing my M.Sc. in Economics at the University of Cologne (expected September 2026). While I prepare PhD applications, I am looking for a research assistant or pre-doctoral position where I can contribute to applied empirical work, and I am writing to ask whether you have such a role open.

I bring hands-on experience with the full applied-micro workflow: my master's thesis (grade: 1.0) built a panel of 558 microregions from ~5 million RAIS administrative observations via SQL/BigQuery and estimated policy effects using heterogeneity-robust difference-in-differences (Callaway & Sant'Anna 2021) in R. Before that, I spent two years at PwC Germany working with large financial datasets. I am comfortable with data construction, cleaning, econometric estimation, and writing up results.

[One line on why this institute/chair specifically: a project, a data source, a policy area.]

I have attached my CV and can provide a writing sample (thesis) or references on request. I am available from [date] and open to a fixed-term or part-time arrangement.
