## Verdict: rejected, on a stated requirement

**This is a field-experiment position, not an applied-micro position.** The project
asks whether digital business technology improves the performance and empowerment
of women entrepreneurs in low-income settings, and the posting says the candidate
*"should expect to spend substantial periods in Kenya during the design, piloting,
baseline, intervention implementation, and follow-up phases."*

The requirement that closes it:

> Demonstrated hands-on experience implementing randomized controlled trials in
> low- or middle-income countries.

Stated as a requirement, not a preference. There is no RCT experience and no
fieldwork experience of any kind. The thesis is observational staggered DiD on an
administrative register, which is a different craft: no baseline survey, no
enumerators, no piloting, no intervention. Sub-Saharan Africa experience and
experience with firms or entrepreneurs are listed as *strongly preferred*, and both
are also absent.

Applying anyway would fail on the first screen and would cost the time that the
Frontier window needs.

## What this posting was worth, which is not nothing

**Two answers that generalise, both verified on Tilburg's own admission pages.**

**A regular academic Master's is enough.** CentER asks for *"an academic Master's or
Research Master's degree in economics, econometrics or another relevant field"*.
Research Master holders *"hold a preferred position"*, so he is admissible but not
preferred. This is the same question left open for Maastricht in
[`../../../phd/international/maastricht.md`](../../../phd/international/maastricht.md),
answered here for Tilburg.

**No GRE, on a condition he already meets.** For an MSc from an EU research
university, CentER wants **at least 24 ECTS in quantitative and research methods
courses** across the BSc and MSc, and the GRE is only the fallback for people who
cannot show that. Advanced Econometrics, Causal Inference, Panel Data Analysis and
Time Series Econometrics clear it on their own. So the no-GRE decision does not
close Tilburg.

**Watch which Tilburg route is being offered.** There are two, and they are not the
same deal.

| Route | What it is |
|---|---|
| Direct PhD, like this vacancy | Salaried employee from day one. The general programme pays about €2.750 gross in year 1 rising to about €3.500 in year 4 |
| Research Master plus PhD | Five years. Two years of Research Master coursework first, then three years of PhD |

The five-year route means starting a second master's after finishing one, which is
not what the October 2027 plan is for. **Check which one a Tilburg posting is before
spending time on it.**

## The general lesson for the Dutch job board

AcademicTransfer carries two different things under the same heading.

**Project positions**, like this one, are attached to a specific grant. They are
fast, well paid and appear without warning, and their requirements come from the
grant rather than from the graduate school, so they can be narrow and are not
negotiable. An RCT project wants someone who has run an RCT.

**General graduate-school intakes** run to the school's own admission rules, which
is where the academic Master's and the 24 ECTS rule apply.

Read the requirement list before the salary. A project position that names a method
he has not used is a reject however good the fit looks on subject.
