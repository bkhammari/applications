## Verdict: a long shot, and the first one worth taking

The three postings before this were rejects on eligibility. This one is not. He is
admissible and weak on one dimension, which is a different situation.

**What makes it worth three days of work.** It **starts in autumn 2026 or by
January 2027**, which is the only position found so far that pays inside the gap
rather than in late 2027. Belgium is inside the PhD geography. No GRE. Energy
Economics is a declared master's focus and the seminar paper is on the EU ETS, a
market instrument for a global public good, which sits next to auction-based
support schemes for renewables. The documents they want almost all exist.

**What makes it a long shot.** They ask for

> solid graduate-level training in microeconomic theory, including industrial
> organization, and game theory

at the level of Mas-Colell, Whinston and Green and of Osborne and Rubinstein. The
coursework is applied econometrics, causal inference, panel data and time series.
Neither industrial organization nor game theory appears in it. **The project is
auction design, which is theory rather than empirics**, so this is the core skill
of the post and not a side requirement.

They also want a two-year research master *"obtained with distinction, or equivalent
qualifications"*. 1,6 is *gut* rather than *sehr gut*, so the distinction claim rests
on top 5 percent of cohort, the Dean's List and the 1,0 thesis, under the
"equivalent qualifications" clause.

He said on 18 September that a change of field is acceptable and that he is
flexible about the doctorate. On that basis this is his call, and the honest framing
is that winning it means doing theory rather than the empirics he is good at.

## One thing to be honest about before applying

The post wants *solid graduate-level training in microeconomic theory*. The
transcript shows **Core Module Microeconomics at 3,0**, and they will see it,
because transcripts are in the required PDF. Mathematics for Economists at 1,0 and
Econometrics at 1,3 pull the other way, but the micro grade is the visible weakness
on exactly the dimension the post names. That is an argument for leading the
statement of intent with the energy coursework and the EU ETS paper rather than with
method.
