## Verdict: rejected, and faster than Tilburg

The required profile, verbatim:

> An MSc degree in (or nearing completion of) an MSc program in **Geoinformatics,
> Computer Science, (Quantitative) Geography, (Spatial / Geographic) Data Science,
> Environmental Science**, or a related discipline; An interest in the impacts of
> land use; Experience in handling spatial data; Programming experience in Python,
> and/or R

Economics is not on that list and is not what "a related discipline" means here.
**Economics and econometrics are not mentioned anywhere in the posting.** The faculty
is Geosciences. The methods are spatial optimisation algorithms, Pareto frontier
computation and uncertainty assessment, none of which he has used.

The project asks how to balance agricultural production against biodiversity
conservation in the Cerrado under future climate scenarios. It is a land-use
modelling post.

## The trap this one sets

**Brazil is the only overlap, and a shared country is not a fit signal.** The thesis
is on the labour market returns to a higher education scholarship, estimated from
RAIS employment records. This project is about where crops and conservation should
go in the Cerrado. Same country, different discipline, different faculty, different
method.

The second false signal is **558 microregions**. That reads as spatial work and it is
not. It is a regional panel, so the geography is an index rather than an object of
analysis. There is no spatial econometrics in the thesis, no GIS, no raster data, no
optimisation.

## What the board actually looks like today

Checked AcademicTransfer on 18 September, filtered to scientific vacancies in
economics and labour economics. **There is nothing in his field open right now.** The
only economics vacancy is the Maastricht Assistant Professor in Macroeconomics,
which needs a doctorate and years of publications. The MILE Inequality Dynamics
round closed on 1 February 2026 and the next one is expected February to April 2027.

That is worth knowing, because it means combing the board daily will keep producing
near-misses like this one and the Tilburg post. The board is a weekly check, not a
daily one, and the effort belongs in the direct enquiries and the Frontier window.
