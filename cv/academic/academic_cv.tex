\resumeItem{Estimated the causal labour-market effects of Brazil's ProUni scholarship programme on 558 microregions (2005--2019) using the Callaway \& Sant'Anna (2021) heterogeneity-robust staggered DiD estimator with doubly-robust inference.}

\resumeItem{Showed that na\"ive TWFE masks positive low-dose effects ($\hat{\beta}_{\text{TWFE}} = -0.65\%$), providing an empirical illustration of forbidden comparisons (Goodman-Bacon, 2021).}
\resumeItem{Heterogeneity analysis: wage gains accrue disproportionately to non-white workers in moderate-exposure markets (consistent with affirmative-action design); gender asymmetry despite majority-female scholarship uptake.}
\resumeItem{Robustness: HonestDiD sensitivity analysis (Rambachan \& Roth, 2023), age-cohort placebo, Bacon decomposition, alternative dose cutoffs, concurrent-policy controls (FIES, Quota Law).}
