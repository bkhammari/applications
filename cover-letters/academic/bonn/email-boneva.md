My research asks which higher education access mechanism — scholarships, loans, or quotas — is most effective at reducing labor market inequality, and for whom. My master's thesis (grade: 1.0), which applied the Callaway & Sant'Anna (2021) staggered DiD estimator to ~5 million administrative observations, found that conventional TWFE estimation masks significant heterogeneity by ethnicity and region.

Your work on perceived returns to education addresses the demand side of the same question my project studies from the supply side. The gender asymmetry I document — persistent wage gaps despite majority-female scholarship uptake — also connects to your ERC project on gender inequality in labor markets.
