My name is Baha Khammari. I am completing my M.Sc. in Economics at the University of Cologne (expected September 2026) and am looking for a doctoral supervisor in applied microeconomics.

My research asks which higher education access mechanism — scholarships, loans, or quotas — is most effective at reducing labour market inequality, and for whom. I am writing to you because your work on how beliefs and perceived returns shape higher education decisions addresses the demand side of the same question my project studies from the supply side. The gender asymmetry I document in my thesis — persistent wage gaps despite majority-female scholarship uptake — also connects to your ERC project on gender inequality in labour markets.
