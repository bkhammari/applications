# Mock numerical reasoning (AD5)

Part of the pass/fail block, and your relative strength. Keep it fast. Each
question has five options. Work from the tables only. Target about 90 seconds per
question. Worked solutions are at the end.

---

### Table A. Scholarships awarded (thousands)

| Programme | 2018 | 2019 | 2020 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ProUni | 200 | 220 | 210 |
| FIES | 300 | 250 | 150 |
| Quota | 100 | 130 | 160 |

**1.** What was the percentage change in FIES awards from 2018 to 2020?
- A. −30%  B. −40%  C. −50%  D. −60%  E. −75%

**2.** In 2020, what share of all awards went to the Quota programme?
- A. 24%  B. 27%  C. 31%  D. 35%  E. 42%

**3.** Which programme increased its awards in every year shown?
- A. ProUni  B. FIES  C. Quota  D. None  E. Cannot be determined

**4.** What was the average number of ProUni awards per year over the three years?
- A. 200k  B. 205k  C. 210k  D. 215k  E. 220k

### Table B. EU traineeship pay

| Institution | Stipend (euro/month) | Duration (months) |
|---|---|---|
| Commission (Blue Book) | 1,390 | 5 |
| EIB | 1,500 | 5 |
| ECB | 1,170 | 6 |

**5.** What is the total stipend paid over a full ECB traineeship?
- A. €5,850  B. €6,950  C. €7,020  D. €7,500  E. €8,400

**6.** How much more does a full EIB traineeship pay than a full Commission traineeship?
- A. €110  B. €330  C. €450  D. €550  E. €700

**7.** A candidate does the Commission traineeship, then the ECB traineeship back to back. What is the total pay across both?
- A. €12,870  B. €13,970  C. €14,020  D. €14,520  E. €15,000

### Gate check

**8.** On the reasoning stage, the numerical and abstract block is scored out of 20 and needs 10 to pass. A candidate gets 7 of 10 numerical and 4 of 10 abstract. Do they clear the gate, and what percentage is their block score?
- A. No, 45%  B. Yes, 50%  C. Yes, 55%  D. Yes, 60%  E. No, 55%

---

## Worked solutions

1. **C.** (150 − 300) / 300 = −150 / 300 = −50%.
2. **C.** 2020 total = 210 + 150 + 160 = 520. Quota share = 160 / 520 = 0.3077, about 31%.
3. **C.** Quota goes 100 → 130 → 160, up each year. ProUni dips in 2020, FIES falls throughout.
4. **C.** (200 + 220 + 210) / 3 = 630 / 3 = 210k.
5. **C.** 1,170 × 6 = €7,020.
6. **D.** EIB 1,500 × 5 = 7,500. Commission 1,390 × 5 = 6,950. Difference = €550.
7. **B.** Commission 6,950 + ECB 7,020 = €13,970.
8. **C.** 7 + 4 = 11, which is at least 10, so the gate is cleared. 11 / 20 = 55%. Note that a strong numerical score (7) carried a weak abstract score (4) over the line, which is exactly the strategy.

Score out of 8. Since this block is only pass/fail, the goal is speed and a safe margin, not perfection.
